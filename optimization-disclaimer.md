## Not a substitute for professional judgment

Cut List 3D helps visualize and plan how parts might fit on sheet stock. It is **not** a substitute for:

- Professional woodworking, cabinetry, or fabrication advice
- Structural engineering or building-code compliance review
- Machine-specific programming or CNC post-processing
- On-site measurement verification

## Verify before you cut

Always verify before cutting material:

- Part and stock dimensions (including thickness and kerf)
- Grain direction and material constraints you entered
- Machine bed size, hold-down, and safety procedures
- That the optimized layout matches your physical stock

Small input errors or solver limitations can produce layouts that do not fit or waste material.

## No guarantee of yield or fit

Optimization results depend on the algorithm, your inputs, and stock assumptions. We do not guarantee minimum waste, perfect nesting, or that every part will fit on available stock.
