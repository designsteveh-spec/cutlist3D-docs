# Privacy Policy — Cut List 3D

**Last updated:** August 23, 2026

**Publisher:** Stephen Heidelberg (where applicable, apps are offered through Naamans Creek Ventures LLC)

**Contact:** designstehev@gmail.com

## Summary

Cut List 3D is an offline-first cut and material layout tool. Your project data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share files.

## Information we collect

We do **not** operate backend servers that collect your projects, parts, stock, or cut layouts. We do **not** require an account.

## Data stored on your device

The app may store locally:

- Project and client names
- Part dimensions, quantities, and labels
- Stock sheet dimensions
- Optimization results and 3D layout previews
- Exported PDF cut sheets in app storage

This data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Permissions

Cut List 3D may request permissions needed to save or share PDF files (for example, storage or sharing intents on Android). The app does not collect location data, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

## Sharing you control

If you export a PDF or use Android’s share sheet, you choose where that file goes. We do not receive copies of shared files.

## Children’s privacy

Cut List 3D is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
